## Optional AI service

Deployments may enable AI explanations of verses through [OpenRouter](https://openrouter.ai/) and the free models it routes to, such as Google's Gemma. They are off by default. Requests go directly from the reader's browser to OpenRouter under OpenRouter's and the model provider's terms. A request contains the verse and its context from the Van Dyck text, the verse's New Arabic Version and KJV text, Greek, Hebrew, or Aramaic words and available glosses/lexicon entries from the study data (depending on the requested task), and any question the reader types. Answers are generated by those models and are not part of the Scripture or study data; this project claims no rights in them. See [docs/development.md](docs/development.md#ai-explanations-optional).
